# Character sprites

64x64 pixel art with 8 facing directions, generated with PixelLab (create-character-v3, low top-down view). Made on a trial account: confirm PixelLab's license terms before a commercial release.

Each bird has its own feather colour, silhouette and signature item, so they stay distinct at board size.

Margaret and Reginald are not used in the game yet (Prodigy boss, review panel).

## Prompts

- **gerald**: a scruffy plump city rock pigeon, ruffled messy grey feathers sticking out, half-closed unimpressed eyes, wearing a worn brown tweed flat cap, iridescent green and purple neck, red feet, full body
- **chick**: a tiny round fluffy bright yellow baby chick wearing a cracked white eggshell as a helmet on its head, huge innocent black eyes, tiny orange beak and feet, full body
- **pigeon**: a lean cocky street pigeon strutting with chest out, glossy dark teal and black feathers, wearing dark black sunglasses, a backwards red baseball cap and a thick gold chain necklace, swagger, pink feet, full body
- **magnus**: a proud pure white pigeon, wearing a tall black top hat and a red scarf with a small white cross, upright regal posture, dark eyes, pale pink feet, full body
- **margaret**: a small neat warm brown and cream pigeon, wearing large round glasses and a white pearl necklace, calm concentrating expression, smooth tidy feathers, full body
- **reginald**: a tall stuffy elderly charcoal black pigeon wearing a brown tweed jacket and a red bow tie, a gold monocle over one eye, a large white press badge pinned to the jacket, deeply disappointed frown, narrow upright posture, full body
