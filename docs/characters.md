# Pigeon Chess: Character Sheet

The flock, by name. Every chess piece has a pigeon behind it, and every pigeon takes the job very seriously.

This sheet follows the art direction's first rule, Deadpan Grandeur: the names are puns, but nobody in the game acknowledges it. A king called Coo-ward is announced with full ceremony.

## How to use this sheet

- **Names and bios** are canon for menus, tooltips, the Collection screen, Reginald's reviews and future story text.
- **Art descriptors** are written in the same prompt format as the existing sprites (`game/src/assets/characters/README.md`), so they can go straight into PixelLab or a similar tool. 64x64, 8 directions, low top-down view, full body.
- **Readability rule.** Each piece pigeon wears a signature item that echoes the chess piece silhouette (crown cross, mitre, tower, helmet plume). At board size the item must say "bishop" before the pigeon says anything at all.
- **Sides.** White pigeons have pale, cream or silver feathers with gold accents. Black pigeons have charcoal or slate feathers with plum or deep red accents. This keeps the two armies apart at a glance, as art direction 5.4 asks.
- **Rendering.** Sprites stay behind the swappable piece layer, so a "Full Pigeon Set" can be a cosmetic piece set (art direction 12) that drops in without code changes.

## The White Court: House Parchment

A respectable flock that roosts in the eaves of a chess club and believes it owns the building.

### King Coo-ward the Confessor (White King)

- **Personality:** Regal, anxious, deeply committed to staying behind other pigeons.
- **Bio:** King Coo-ward has never lost a battle, because he has never attended one. He considers castling the finest move in chess and does it at every opportunity, including twice in one game when nobody was watching.
- **Line:** "We shall lead from the back. As is tradition."
- **Art descriptor:** a plump pale cream and silver pigeon wearing a tall gold crown topped with a small cross, a heavy ermine cape dragging behind him, nervous wide eyes, slightly hunched posture as if about to hide, iridescent green neck, pink feet, full body

### Queen Featherine the Great (White Queen)

- **Personality:** Tireless, decisive, faintly disappointed in her husband.
- **Bio:** Featherine does the work of eight pigeons and the paperwork of nine. She moves in every direction because she has somewhere to be in every direction. Her crown is taller than the King's. Nobody mentions it.
- **Line:** "Stand aside, dear. I'll handle it."
- **Art descriptor:** a tall elegant white pigeon with silver-tipped wings, wearing a tall ornate gold crown with many points and pearls, a high lace collar, sharp confident eyes, upright commanding posture, iridescent purple neck, pink feet, full body

### Bishop Pecksniff (White Bishop)

- **Personality:** Pious, judgemental, will not walk in a straight line.
- **Bio:** The Right Reverend Pecksniff believes the straight path is for the unenlightened. He moves only on the diagonal and blesses breadcrumbs before eating them, which takes a long time.
- **Line:** "Bless this crumb. And that one. And that one."
- **Art descriptor:** a thin pale grey pigeon wearing a tall white and gold bishop's mitre leaning slightly to one side, long white robes with a gold stole, small round spectacles, a disapproving squint, pink feet, full body

### Sir Lance-a-Coo (White Knight)

- **Personality:** Gallant, dramatic, has never moved in a straight line in his life.
- **Bio:** Sir Lance-a-Coo jumps over his own allies to reach the enemy and calls it chivalry. He does not own a horse. He wears a helmet shaped like one and hopes nobody asks.
- **Line:** "Over the pawns! Into glory! Mind your heads."
- **Art descriptor:** a sturdy light grey pigeon wearing a silver knight's helmet shaped like a horse head with the visor up, a small white tabard with a gold cross, a tiny lance held under one wing, heroic puffed chest, pink feet, full body

### Lady Roostwell of the Keep (White Rook)

- **Personality:** Immovable, sensible, extremely protective of corners.
- **Bio:** Lady Roostwell has held the same corner square since the opening and sees no reason to leave until the endgame. She is not a rook. She is aware rooks are birds. She would like that to stop coming up.
- **Line:** "I am a pigeon. The tower is a hat."
- **Art descriptor:** a broad square-shouldered cream and fawn pigeon wearing a stone castle tower as a hat with crenellations on top, a small heraldic shield on her chest, calm stern expression, solid planted stance, pink feet, full body

### Private Pecks and the Squab Squad (White Pawns)

- **Personality:** Brave, small, numerous, slightly confused about the plan.
- **Bio:** Eight young pigeons who were promised that one of them would become a queen. They march forward and never back. Private Pecks leads the squad because he was standing at the front.
- **Line:** "Forward! Only forward. We were told there'd be a promotion."
- **Art descriptor:** a small round young pale grey pigeon wearing a little white paper soldier hat, a tiny wooden shield, determined eyes, stubby wings at its sides, standing at attention, pink feet, full body

## The Black Flock: The Ink Murmuration

A flock that roosts under the clock tower and insists it is not sinister, only well dressed.

### Emperor Coo-lius Caesar (Black King)

- **Personality:** Grand, suspicious, trusts no pigeon, least of all his bishops.
- **Bio:** Coo-lius came, he saw, he stayed on his starting square for forty moves. He has read every chess book ever written and believes all of them are about him. He is wary of anyone named Brutus, and of any pawn on the seventh rank.
- **Line:** "Et tu, pawn?"
- **Art descriptor:** a large dark charcoal pigeon wearing a gold laurel wreath around a small dark crown with a cross, a deep purple toga draped over one shoulder, narrowed suspicious eyes, proud raised chin, iridescent purple neck, dark red feet, full body

### Queen Coo-leopatra (Black Queen)

- **Personality:** Glamorous, ruthless, always three moves ahead and bored by it.
- **Bio:** Coo-leopatra captures with style and leaves a single dark feather behind. Her eyeliner is perfect. Her diagonals are perfect. She once took a rook and both of its knights in one evening and called it a light supper.
- **Line:** "Bring me the next one."
- **Art descriptor:** a sleek slate black pigeon with glossy plum-tinted feathers, wearing a tall golden crown with a cobra crest, heavy dark eyeliner, a broad gold and turquoise collar necklace, elegant posture with one wing raised, dark red feet, full body

### Friar Tuck-in (Black Bishop)

- **Personality:** Jolly, hungry, surprisingly vicious on the long diagonal.
- **Bio:** Friar Tuck-in took a vow of poverty and a separate, stronger vow of breakfast. He is always eating. He slides across the board at speed, mostly toward food, and captures pieces mainly because they were in the way.
- **Line:** "Is anyone finishing that rook?"
- **Art descriptor:** a round chubby dark grey pigeon in a brown hooded monk's robe tied with a rope belt, a black bishop's mitre perched on the hood, a half-eaten bread roll held under one wing, crumbs on his chest, cheerful eyes, dark red feet, full body

### Sir Pidgeval the Unhorsed (Black Knight)

- **Personality:** Brooding, loyal, prone to sudden L-shaped exits.
- **Bio:** Sir Pidgeval lost his horse in a forfeit and has worn its helmet ever since. He speaks rarely. He never moves straight. Opponents say he appears where he should not be, and leaves before anyone can argue.
- **Line:** "..."
- **Art descriptor:** a lean dark slate pigeon wearing a black iron knight's helmet shaped like a horse head with a tall deep red plume, a tattered black cape, a small dark shield, glowing pale eyes behind the visor, tense ready stance, dark red feet, full body

### Count Rookula (Black Rook)

- **Personality:** Aristocratic, nocturnal, does not enjoy daylight or open files.
- **Bio:** Count Rookula has lived in the same dark tower for centuries. He only comes out in the endgame, when the board is empty and the shadows are long. He does not drink blood. He drinks the opponent's time on the clock.
- **Line:** "I never castle. I am the castle."
- **Art descriptor:** a tall pale grey and black pigeon with a widow's peak of dark feathers, wearing a high-collared black cape with a deep red lining, a small dark stone castle tower worn as a hat, pale stern face, two tiny pointed beak tips, folded wings, dark red feet, full body

### Corporal Crumb and the Grey Area (Black Pawns)

- **Personality:** Grim, disciplined, secretly hopeful.
- **Bio:** Eight pigeons from the clock tower who march in perfect step and speak only in whispers. Corporal Crumb has been a pawn for a long time. He knows the way to the eighth rank. He has never made it. This time feels different.
- **Line:** "Steady. One square at a time."
- **Art descriptor:** a small round young dark charcoal pigeon wearing a little black iron helmet with a short spike, a tiny dark shield, serious narrow eyes, upright soldier stance, dark red feet, full body

## Special pieces

These appear through Asymmetric Armies (Category E) modifiers. Their side colouring follows the player who drafted them.

### Chancellor Featherstonehaugh (Chancellor, E004)

- **Personality:** Bureaucratic, versatile, impossible to pronounce. It is said "Fanshaw".
- **Bio:** Half rook, half knight, entirely paperwork. The Chancellor moves straight or jumps, depending on which form was filed first. He has never been on time and has never been late.
- **Line:** "I'll need that in triplicate."
- **Art descriptor:** a stout grey pigeon wearing a stone tower hat with a small horse head carving rising from the top, a long black chancellor's robe with gold trim, a quill tucked behind one wing, half-moon spectacles, a rolled scroll, pink feet, full body

### Sergeant Coo-rage (Royal Guard, E003)

- **Personality:** Loyal, loud, believes every square is a threat.
- **Bio:** Sergeant Coo-rage protects the King at all costs, and mostly from the King's own decisions. When captured, she bursts into silver confetti, which she considers a dignified exit.
- **Line:** "Nobody touches His Majesty. Not even His Majesty."
- **Art descriptor:** a sturdy silver grey pigeon wearing a tall bearskin guard hat and a red tunic with silver buttons, a small round silver shield with a crown emblem, stern unblinking eyes, rigid at attention, pink feet, full body

The modifier pigeon is the existing **Pigeon** character. No new design. Its dotted hop arrow and nameplate stay as in art direction 5.6.
